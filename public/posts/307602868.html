水蛭（Leech）[Anki, SuperMemo]

#水蛭卡片#间隔重复#知识表述#助记法#记忆连贯性

Leeches in SuperMemo are questions that a student keeps forgetting[1]. Most often leeches come from disrespect for the 20 rules of knowledge formulation. Some well-formulated material may also need a boost from mnemonic techniques or a boost in coherence, e.g. by setting questions in relevant context.

SuperMemo 中的 Leech 指的是学生经常遗忘的问题。大多数情况下，Leech 来自对知识表述的 20 条规则[2]的不尊重。一些表述良好的材料可能还需要加强助记技巧或增强连贯性[3]，例如在相关的上下文设置问题。
